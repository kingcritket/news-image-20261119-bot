One image summing up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026: Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen in the first-ever trilateral at the APEC summit; Iran hits a U.S. base in Kuwait, targeting the Patriot site at Ali Al Salem, as Bahrain intercepts another attack; and Grand Theft Auto VI rolls out worldwide after a 13-year wait. Also today: Democrats pick Hakeem Jeffries as Speaker nominee (251–11), Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast with landfall due 20 November, NASA briefs on Voyager 1's one-light-day milestone, and India and Japan hold 2+2 defence talks in Tokyo.

All stories verified against reporting from 19 November 2026 (Yonhap, Reuters, AP, NASA JPL, IMD, Axios). Three AI-generated scene photos with no text baked in, composited with PIL; every word on the image is drawn by PIL in Liberation Sans Bold. The image is built by `build_entry.py` and shipped as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

To rebuild the image from the scene photos:

```sh
python3 build_entry.py
```
